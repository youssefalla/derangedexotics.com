About Deranged Exotics Prompt

Deranged Exotics Prompt is a free tool that helps entrepreneurs, freelancers, and business owners create perfect AI prompts for building their websites — in seconds, with no coding required.

What We Do

We guide you through 5 simple questions about your website needs, then generate a detailed, professional prompt that you can paste into any AI website builder like Lovable, Bolt, v0, or AntiGravity AI Studio.

Why Deranged Exotics Prompt?

  • 🚀 Save hours writing prompts from scratch
  • 🎨 Get professionally structured prompts optimized for AI builders
  • 🌍 Available in English, French, and Arabic
  • 💯 Completely free — no signup required
  • ⚡ Generate unlimited prompts instantly

How It Works

Answer 5 questions: describe your website, choose your business type, pick a design style, select which sections you want, and choose your call-to-action. Deranged Exotics Prompt assembles a detailed, conversion-optimized prompt ready to use in any AI builder.
